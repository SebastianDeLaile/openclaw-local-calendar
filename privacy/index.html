Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 September 2026

Purpose

OpenClaw Local Calendar is a private, personal tool used only by the owner of delaile.com. It accesses Google Calendar data only after the owner grants permission through Google OAuth.

Data accessed and use

The tool may access calendar lists, event details, attendees, reminders, and related calendar metadata. It uses this data only to list, search, create, update, or delete calendar events at the owner's request.

Storage and processing

OAuth credentials are stored locally on the owner's Mac. Calendar information may be processed by the owner's locally configured OpenClaw installation and its configured service providers when the owner requests calendar assistance. The tool does not sell calendar data, use it for advertising, or use it to train its own models.

Sharing

Calendar data is not shared with unrelated third parties. It is disclosed only as needed to Google, OpenClaw, and service providers configured by the owner to perform the requested calendar operation.

Retention and security

The tool does not maintain a separate public calendar database. Local credentials and configuration are protected using operating-system file permissions. Calendar data remains subject to the retention and security practices of Google and any service providers explicitly configured by the owner.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke access at any time from the Google Account third-party connections page and can delete locally stored OAuth credentials.

Contact

Privacy enquiries can be directed to the owner through the contact links at delaile.com.
